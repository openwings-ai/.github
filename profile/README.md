We build AI tools for Brazilian businesses that serve customers on WhatsApp and Instagram.

## Products

- [atendi.me](https://atendi.me): answer customers with your business's prices and opening hours, and book appointments.
- [iLojista](https://ilojista.com.br): handle the first questions at your iPhone store, using your own catalog. Collect trade-in details and book visits when you enable scheduling.
- [Prospectar](https://prospectar.io): reach out to prospects, book meetings and follow up. AI and your sales team share the same conversations.

All three use [@falai/agent](https://falai.dev), our open-source TypeScript library for AI conversations controlled by your code.

## Connect your AI tools

Read each product's catalog and sales stages from Claude Code, Codex, ChatGPT or Cursor. See [plugins and setup steps](https://github.com/openwings-ai/plugins).
